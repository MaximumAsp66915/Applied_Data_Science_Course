What it is

One Telegram group. Every song it ever shared.

SUT Music began inside SharifMusic, a Telegram group that grew from an Electrical Engineering hangout into a university-wide music community. Two scraping rebuilds and a full database redesign later, every shared track, artist, and reaction lives in one Postgres pipeline — and a Telegram Mini App lets anyone in the group play, rank, and get suggestions from it, with zero login and zero separate account.

The recommendation engine

Six ways the app decides what plays next

One artist-first hybrid model powers most of it, re-ranked by a lighter track-level model, and every other list on the app doubles as a suggestion surface in its own right.

Why artists, not tracks: an early track-level matrix factorization model plateaued at recall@10 ≈ 0.03–0.04 on a 1.7%-dense matrix. Collapsing the same signal onto 2,910 artists instead of 9,900 tracks produced a much denser matrix and pushed recall@10 to 0.087. The shipped configuration — 5 candidate artists, 1 track each, track-model re-ranking on — trades a little raw recall for far better variety, landing at Recall@1 = 0.038.
